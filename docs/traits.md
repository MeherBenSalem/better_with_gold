# Traits

Every recruit rolls one or two traits from its class's list; the first one gives its epithet.

| Trait | Effect | Epithets |
|---|---|---|
| **Steadfast** | +10% health and +20% knockback resistance. | the Steadfast, Ironroot |
| **Swift** | +12% movement speed. | the Swift, Quickstep |
| **Brutal** | +15% damage. | the Brutal, Bonebreaker |
| **Keen-eyed** | +15% damage and +2 blocks of range with bows and spells. | Hawkeye, the Keen |
| **Stalwart** | Bleeds out 50% slower when downed. | the Unbroken, Last-Stand |
| **Devoted** | Morale never drops below 40. | the Faithful, Oathkeeper |
| **Lucky** | 10% chance to shrug off a hit. | the Lucky, Fortune's Own |
| **Greedy** | Costs 30% more gold, deals 10% more damage. | Goldgrasp, the Greedy |
| **Hardy** | Regains 1 health every 4 seconds out of combat. | the Hardy, Stonehide |
| **Fearless** | Loses no morale when allies fall. | the Fearless, Dauntless |
| **Merciful** | +25% healing done. | the Kind, Mercy |
