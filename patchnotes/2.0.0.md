# Remnants | Fellowship 2.0.0

Better With Gold is now **Remnants | Fellowship**: hire named sellswords with gold and lead them as a warband.
The mod id is still `better_with_gold`, so 1.x worlds keep their gold gear.

Supported: **1.20.1** (Fabric, Forge), **1.21.1** (Fabric, NeoForge, Forge), **26.2** and **26.3** (Fabric,
NeoForge). Fabric builds need Fabric API; nothing else is required.

## Sellswords and the warband

- Sellswords for hire turn up near village bells. Each has a name, a class, a level, one or two traits and a price
  in gold ingots shown above their head. Use them while holding gold to hire them.
- Five classes: **Bulwark** (tank, taunts enemies off everyone else), **Skirmisher** (hits every enemy around it),
  **Ranger** (keeps its distance, volleys), **Mender** (heals the most hurt ally or leader, revives the downed) and
  **Arcanist** (arc bolts, a storm on packed enemies). Classes are JSON files packs can edit or add to.
- Eleven traits (Steadfast, Swift, Brutal, Keen-eyed, Stalwart, Devoted, Lucky, Greedy, Hardy, Fearless,
  Merciful), levels 1 to 20 with titles, and morale.
- Up to 6 allies per leader by default (configurable up to 24).

## Leading

- **Command wheel (hold G):** Follow, Charge, Focus, Hold, Fall back, Guard banner, Formation and Recall.
- **Formations:** Line, Wedge, Shield Wall (tanks and melee in front) and Circle.
- **Warbanner:** plant it and the warband forms up around it and guards it; lift it and they follow you again.
- **Aggro:** enemies attack whoever holds the most aggro, and only switch when someone beats it by 10%. Bulwarks
  taunt; a label above each engaged enemy shows who it is after.
- **Roster (U):** every ally's stats, traits, ability and Chronicle; send allies to camp, summon or dismiss them;
  the Remembrance of the fallen.
- **Party frames** in the top-left corner with health and downed countdowns.

## Downed, not dead

- At zero health an ally collapses instead of dying, enemies lose interest, and it bleeds out over 30 seconds.
  Crouch beside it to revive it; Menders revive by themselves.
- A bled-out ally recovers at camp for 5 minutes. Optional permadeath sends the fallen to the Remembrance.

## The Chronicle

Each ally's story is written from what it did: where it swore the oath, its first kill, kill milestones, bosses
fought and slain, the times it went down and who pulled it back, the dimensions it crossed into.

## Never lost

Following allies travel with you through log-outs and dimension changes, and Recall finds allies left in unloaded
areas by loading the chunk where they were last seen.

## Gold

- Hiring costs gold ingots. Each piece of Reinforced Golden Armour you wear adds 3% damage and 5 minimum morale to
  allies near you. The Golden Fish fully heals an ally and adds 30 morale.
- The 17 gold items are kept with their behaviour, now in the Gilded Regalia tab, and their crafting and smithing
  recipes (missing since 1.2) are back.

## Compatibility

- Optional link to RPG Attribute System: allies cannot out-level your RPG level (at least level 5).
- Allies are vanilla owned entities, which Remnants: RPG Mobs, Skills and Powers and Mob Loot Bags treat as yours.
